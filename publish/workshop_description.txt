[h1]JDeathmatch[/h1]
[b]Fast, no-nonsense free-for-all deathmatch.[/b] Pick your loadout, rack up kills, unleash the MOAB, be the first to the kill limit. Every weapon, speed and rule is configurable, and it works with any weapon pack.

[h2]How to play[/h2]
[list]
[*] Pick [b]JDeathmatch[/b] from the gamemode list (bottom right of the main menu) and start a map, ideally a [i]dm_[/i] map.
[*] Choose your weapons in the loadout menu, then fight.
[*] First player to reach the kill limit (default [b]30[/b]) wins the match.
[/list]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Loadout menu[/b] (press [b]Q[/b]): one primary, one secondary and two special slots. Changes apply on your next spawn.
[*] [b]Any weapon pack[/b]: HL2, M9K, CW, TFA... the weapon list is a simple JSON file.
[*] [b]Per-weapon balancing[/b]: set the starting ammo and a damage multiplier for each weapon (also for grenades and rockets).
[*] [b]MOAB killstreak[/b]: reach the kill threshold to become the [b]Annihilator[/b] and get a random super weapon for a limited time.
[*] [b]Slow-motion finale[/b]: the winning kill slows down time, then everyone spectates the winner.
[*] [b]Map vote[/b] at the end of every match ([i]dm_[/i] and [i]ttt_[/i] maps).
[*] [b]Scoreboard[/b] (hold [b]Tab[/b]) with kills, deaths, K/D, MOAB status and progress to win.
[*] [b]Custom HUD[/b]: health, armor, ammo and kills to win, a weapon selector grouped by loadout slot and a kill feed with your weapon names (and headshots).
[*] Works with [b]bots[/b], perfect for testing your setup.
[/list]

[h2]Settings[/h2]
Set them in the [b]Start New Game[/b] menu, in [i]server.cfg[/i] or live from the console:
[table]
[tr][th]ConVar[/th][th]Default[/th][th]Description[/th][/tr]
[tr][td]jdm_playerhealth[/td][td]100[/td][td]Player health[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]jdm_playerwalkspeed[/td][td]100[/td][td]Walk speed (%)[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]jdm_playerrunspeed[/td][td]100[/td][td]Sprint speed (%)[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]jdm_gravity[/td][td]100[/td][td]Gravity (%), lower = higher jumps[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]jdm_maxkills[/td][td]30[/td][td]Kills to win the match[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]jdm_moabkills[/td][td]25[/td][td]Kills to get the MOAB (keep it below jdm_maxkills)[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]jdm_moabtimer[/td][td]30[/td][td]MOAB duration in seconds[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]jdm_flashlight[/td][td]1[/td][td]Allow the flashlight[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]jdm_farspawn[/td][td]0[/td][td]Spawn far from enemies[/td][/tr]

[tr][td]jdm_winmusic[/td][td]CP Violation[/td][td]Victory music, any sound path[/td][/tr]
[/table]

[h2]Custom weapons[/h2]
The weapon list lives in [b]garrysmod/data/jdmw.txt[/b] and is created with HL2 weapons on first start. Each entry looks like this:
[code]{ "id": "weapon_ar2", "name": "AR2", "slot": 1, "ammo": 120, "dmgmul": 1.0 }[/code]
[list]
[*] [b]slot[/b]: 1 = primary, 2 = secondary, 3 = special (also selectable in slot 4), 4 = second special only, [b]666 = MOAB weapon[/b]
[*] [b]ammo[/b]: total ammo on spawn (0 = weapon default)
[*] [b]dmgmul[/b]: damage multiplier (1 = normal, 0.5 = half, 2 = double)
[/list]
After editing, run [b]jdm_reloadWeaponsConfig[/b] from the server console (or as admin) to apply it without restarting.
Or skip the file: admins can press [b]F3[/b] in game to edit damage, ammo and slots, and add or remove weapons from a searchable list of installed ones. The Settings tab changes every ConVar above live.
Full guide: [url=https://github.com/JDReachDev/JDeathmatch]GitHub[/url]

[h2]Dedicated servers[/h2]
Add this addon (and the weapon packs you use) to your server's Workshop collection, so players download everything automatically, then start with [b]+gamemode jdeathmatch[/b].

[h2]Known limitations[/h2]
[list]
[*] Free-for-all only, no teams.
[*] Addons that override spawning or loadouts may conflict.
[/list]
